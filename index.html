Four lines of work, all industries

Each engagement starts with one question: is there a real reason for both sides to talk? If there isn't, we say so.

01

Capital introduction

For companies seeking investment

02

Buy-side search

For investors deploying capital

We find and qualify targets against a written mandate and bring forward the ones that meet it.

Sell-side

The name

יכין
Yakin is the Hebrew name of the right-hand pillar at the entrance to Solomon's Temple (1 Kings 7:21), traditionally understood as "he establishes".

In standard gematria יכין equals 90. The mark translates that into a central pillar, two supports and an upward point. "Capital" carries a double meaning: financial capital, and the architectural capital that crowns a column.
