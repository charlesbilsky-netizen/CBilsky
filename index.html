Four lines of work, all industries

Capital introduction

For companies seeking investment. We assess readiness, tighten the story, map investor fit, and make introductions only where there is a reason for both sides to talk.

Buy-side search

For investors deploying capital. We find and qualify targets against a written mandate and bring forward the ones that meet it.

Sell-side

For owners preparing to sell. We prepare the business for a buyer's questions, identify counterparties, and manage the process to a signed term sheet.

Referrals

Where another adviser already holds the right mandate, we make the introduction and step back. The right seat matters more than the fee.

The name

יכין
Yakin is the Hebrew name of the right-hand pillar at the entrance to Solomon's Temple (1 Kings 7:21), traditionally understood as "he establishes".

In standard gematria יכין equals 90. The mark translates that into a central pillar, two supports and an upward point. "Capital" carries a double meaning: financial capital, and the architectural capital that crowns a column.
