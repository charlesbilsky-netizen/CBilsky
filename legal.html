Privacy notice

Controller: Yakin Capital Charles Bilsky, Warsaw, Poland, contact as above.

What we collect: the details you send by email (name, company, email address, and what you write). Nothing is collected automatically beyond standard server logs held by the hosting provider.

Why: to answer your enquiry and, if you engage us, to carry out that engagement. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legitimate interest in running the business (Article 6(1)(f)).
